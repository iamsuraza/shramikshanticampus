Academic Programs

Pokhara University programs focused on artificial intelligence, information technology and law.

BAIDS

Bachelor of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science

A future-facing program for students who want to build careers in AI, analytics, automation and data-driven decision making.

Applying Criteria

  • 10+2 from any stream with 100 marks in Computer Science or Mathematics
  • Grade 9-12 Technical Education with 100 marks in Mathematics or Computer
  • A-Level with minimum Grade D

Career Pathways

  • AI and machine learning assistant
  • Data analyst
  • Software and automation developer
  • Research and analytics associate
BCSIT

Bachelor of Computer System and Information Technology

A computing program designed for students from any stream who want a strong foundation in systems, software, networking and IT operations.

Eligibility

  • Students from any stream can apply
  • Interest in computing, systems and problem solving is recommended

Career Pathways

  • Software developer
  • System administrator
  • Network support engineer
  • IT officer
BA LL.B

Bachelor of Arts Bachelor of Laws

An integrated law program for students preparing for legal practice, governance, advocacy, public administration and research.

Eligibility

  • Students from any stream can apply
  • Strong reading, writing and public reasoning skills are helpful

Career Pathways

  • Legal practitioner
  • Legal advisor
  • Public service officer
  • Rights and policy researcher
