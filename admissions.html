Admissions 2081 BS

Applications are open for BCSIT, BALLB, and BAIDS programs. Join over 1,200 students building their futures at Shramik Shanti Campus.

How to Apply

Admission Process

Follow these four simple steps to secure your place at Shramik Shanti Campus.

1

Submit Inquiry

Fill in the online inquiry form below or visit the campus admissions office in person.

2

Document Verification

Submit all required documents to the admissions office for verification and review.

3

Pay Admission Fee

Complete payment of the admission and registration fee at the campus accounts office.

4

Collect ID Card

Collect your student identity card and attend the orientation program to begin your studies.

Requirements

Required Documents

Please prepare the following documents before visiting the admissions office.

📄

SEE / SLC Certificate

Original + 2 photocopies of your School Leaving Certificate

📜

+2 / Equivalent Certificate

Original + 2 photocopies of your 10+2 or equivalent marksheet

🪪

Citizenship Certificate

Self-attested photocopy of your Nepal citizenship certificate

📸

Passport-Size Photos

4 recent passport-size photographs with white background

📋

Migration Certificate

Required for students from institutions outside Kathmandu Valley

🧾

Character Certificate

Character certificate from your previous educational institution
