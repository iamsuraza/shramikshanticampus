About Shramik Shanti Campus

A constituent campus of Pokhara University in Chyasal-9, Lalitpur, Nepal.

Our Story

Community values with university-level ambition.

Established in 2007, Shramik Shanti Campus (SSC), located in Chyasal, Lalitpur, is a constituent campus of Pokhara University. Originally a community-based, non-profit institution affiliated with Tribhuvan University offering BBS and MBS programs, it transitioned to Pokhara University in 2025 and expanded its academic offerings to include programs in Law, Information Technology, and Artificial Intelligence.

The campus aims to deliver quality education that prepares students for professional success while fostering academic excellence, innovation and social responsibility.
