Admission Process

Simple steps to start.

1. Contact Campus

Call 015404111 or submit the inquiry form.

2. Confirm Eligibility

Choose BAIDS, BCSIT or BA LL.B and verify your documents.

3. Visit Office

Bring academic documents, photos and citizenship copy.

4. Enroll

Complete admission and attend orientation.

Admission Inquiry

Documents

Prepare these documents

SEE / SLC certificate
+2 or equivalent certificate
Citizenship copy
Passport-size photos
Migration certificate if required
Character certificate
