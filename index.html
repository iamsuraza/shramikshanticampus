About SSC

Rooted in access. Focused on future-ready education.

Established in 2007, Shramik Shanti Campus is located in Chyasal, Lalitpur. It began as a community-based, non-profit institution and became a constituent campus of Pokhara University in 2025, expanding into Law, Information Technology and Artificial Intelligence.

  • Constituent Campus of Pokhara University
  • Located at Chyasal-9, Lalitpur, near Shankhamul Park
  • Programs under Management, Science and Technology, and Humanities and Social Sciences
